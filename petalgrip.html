Role

Where

Project Background

SolidWorks course project focused on redesigning cervical biopsy forceps to improve tissue capture and retention.

Cervical biopsies rely on high-quality tissue samples for pathological evaluation, making reliable specimen collection critical for early diagnosis.

Existing forceps can lose tissue during withdrawal and produce variable biopsy depth, which can reduce sample quality and lead to repeat procedures.

My Contribution

  • Developed the forceps concept in SolidWorks, creating detailed part models, assemblies, and engineering drawings.
  • Independently modeled a foldable net mechanism in SolidWorks to simulate the proposed tissue-retention design.
  • Helped fabricate prototypes and iterated the design through simulation and functional testing.
  • Documented the full design process through step-by-step CAD tutorials and a comprehensive engineering design report covering design rationale, prototyping, testing, materials, manufacturing, and cost analysis.

Challenges & Key Decisions

  • Modeling a foldable net mechanism that could open, collapse, and fit within the forceps geometry.
  • Determining how to securely integrate the net into the physical forceps prototype.

Project Outcomes

Completed and submitted a final forceps prototype supported by a comprehensive engineering design report.
