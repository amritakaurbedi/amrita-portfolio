Laparoscopic Trainer Lab Module

Role
Team Lead
When
January 2026 – June 2026

Project Background

UCI lacked an undergraduate BME lab experience focused on regulatory compliance and the testing used to demonstrate that a medical device meets a standard.

Developed a hands-on module for an ABET-accredited BME lab course to introduce students to experimental design, engineering testing, and regulatory evaluation.

My Contribution

  • Designed and executed Arduino-based benchtop protocols to quantify laparoscopic tool–tissue interaction forces and developed MATLAB analyses to interpret the resulting data.
  • Created SOPs, instructional materials, and quantitative analyses for the lab module.
  • Piloted the module with 20+ undergraduate students, tracking task-completion time and collecting survey feedback to identify usability issues and refine the lab experience before course implementation.
  • Co-authored the conference paper documenting the module and its experimental and regulatory-testing framework.

Results

  • Refined the lab module through pilot testing and student feedback before classroom implementation.
  • Confirmed that the laparoscopic trainers met the applicable ISO 7741 performance requirements through quantitative testing.
  • Module planned for implementation in the BME laboratory course this academic year.
  • Published the work in the American Society for Engineering Education (ASEE) conference paper.

Key takeaways

  • Reading and understanding technical standards well enough to apply them to testing.
  • Learning how to turn a written standard into an actual test plan.
  • Designing a lab that students with different levels of experience could follow.
