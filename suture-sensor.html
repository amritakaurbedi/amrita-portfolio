The short version

Custom PCB pressure sensor for biomedical force monitoring — 0–183 kPa, R² = 0.997, 0.20 kPa resolution, 26.5 dB SNR.

Context

Building the sensor was the easy half. The harder half was characterising it honestly: resolution, repeatability, signal-to-noise, and residual error, documented well enough that someone else could trust the numbers.

What I did

  • Build and calibrate a custom PCB-based pressure sensor for biomedical force monitoring — 0–183 kPa range, R² = 0.997, 0.046% residual error.
  • Run hardware validation in MATLAB, reaching 0.20 kPa resolution and 26.5 dB SNR, while evaluating repeatability, accuracy, and process performance.
  • Write and implement the SOPs for fabrication, testing, and design verification under quality control standards.
  • Present at the 2025 National BMES Conference and the UCI Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program Symposium.
