Internship

Quality Engineering Internship at Applied Medical

When
June 2026 – September 2026

Project Background

Worked within Quality Systems in a regulated medical-device environment, with most of the internship focused on CAPA investigations.

My Contribution

  • Developed investigation plans for two cross-functional CAPAs, met with process owners and stakeholders, reviewed technical documentation, and evaluated evidence to determine likely root causes.
  • Built an I-MR chart to evaluate failure frequency and process behavior, wrote CAPA investigation proposals, presented findings to senior leadership, and incorporated feedback into the investigation approach.
  • Collaborated with another intern to redesign a package peel-test fixture in SolidWorks to better reflect real-world package opening; created engineering drawings and completed an initial prototype/testing round with the Packaging team.
  • Supported Clinical Development by reviewing and verifying competitor claims across energy-device portfolios.
  • Participated in weekly automation learning sessions covering PLCs, HMIs, ladder logic, robotics, and industrial controls.

Results

  • One CAPA was closed after the evidence supported a non-systemic issue, while the second progressed into corrective action after a procedural gap was identified.
  • Developed a preliminary process-improvement plan for the second CAPA, outlining potential corrective steps for continued validation and implementation after the internship.
  • Presented CAPA findings and peel-test fixture development to cross-functional stakeholders and senior leadership.
  • Delivered a redesigned fixture concept, engineering drawings, and initial prototype for continued evaluation by the Packaging team.

Key takeaways

  • Learned to go beyond the documentation, speak with the people closest to the process, and follow the evidence even when it challenged the original assumption.
  • Gained familiarity with ISO 13485 and 21 CFR Part 820 and how those requirements shape quality-system investigations and decision-making.
  • Gained exposure to industrial controls, an area I had no prior background in and became interested in learning more about.
