MedTech Founders Program

BMES Project Coordinator · Co-Founder, MedTech Founders Program · 2025–Present

What started on a whiteboard became the MedTech Founders Program (MFP): a year-long product development pipeline helping undergraduate biomedical engineers turn ideas into medical innovations across Ideation, Business, and Prototyping. Coming into college, my co-leads and I saw a lack of support for students who wanted to build something of their own in the medical device space. As BMES Project Coordinators, we created MFP from the ground up to fill that gap. In its first year, we mentored 13 teams and connected students with faculty and industry mentors as they moved from early ideas to pitches and physical prototypes.

Watching those ideas grow into real projects has been one of the most rewarding parts of building MFP together.
